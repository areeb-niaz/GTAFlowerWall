Questions

What everything costs, how far we travel, and how the day runs. If your question is not here, call or text and we will answer it straight.

Prices and booking

How much does it cost?

The Essential Nikkah Wall is $200 and the Signature Stage Package is $250. Add-ons are the LED arch welcome mirror at $200, a foam board welcome sign at $125, floral urns with candles at $30 and a white ceremony rug at $35. Every price is on the menu page.

What is included in the price?

Delivery, setup before the ceremony, and takedown at the end of the night, even when the venue runs past midnight. In Mississauga and Brampton that is all included, with nothing added on the day.

What is the difference between Essential and Signature?

Essential is the wall: a pipe-and-drape backdrop with a floral header across the top, chiffon side curtains and cascading petal strands through the centre. Signature adds a white ceremony rug and two floral urns with LED pillar candles, for $15 less than adding them separately.

Can I book just the wall?

Yes. The wall on its own is $200. Everything else is optional.

Someone else quoted $150. Why is yours more?

Lower quotes are usually a bare divider with no floral header, rug or candles, and often no takedown. Compare what actually arrives and what is included, not the headline number.

How do I hold my date?

Send your date through the booking form or call or text 647-471-2148. A $50 e-transfer holds the date, and the balance is paid in cash on the day.

The wall

Why is the partition see-through?

By design. The couple walks through it and sits on either side, which is how the ceremony photographs best.

Can the same wall be the partition and the photo backdrop?

Yes, that is what it is built for. It separates the two sides during the nikkah, then becomes the wall everyone photographs afterwards. It reads clean in photos and video, in daylight or in hall lighting.

Will the wall damage the venue?

No. It is freestanding on a narrow base, and nothing is fixed to walls, carpet or fittings. There is nothing to patch, unscrew or clean off after we leave.

How is it placed in a prayer hall?

We position it to respect the prayer hall layout and keep the walkways clear. Because it is freestanding, the placement can be adjusted on the day to suit the room.

Delivery and travel

Do you deliver to Brampton?

Yes. Delivery, setup and takedown are free in Brampton, the same as in Mississauga.

Do you travel to Toronto or Vaughan?

Yes. Outside Mississauga and Brampton we quote one all-in price with travel included, before you book.

We travel to Toronto, Etobicoke, Scarborough, Oakville, Milton, Burlington, Hamilton, Vaughan, Markham, Richmond Hill, Caledon, Halton Hills, Ajax, Pickering, Whitby and Oshawa.

We have set up at ISNA Canada in Mississauga and Artistic Decor Venue in Etobicoke.

Do I need to set anything up or take it down myself?

No. We agree an arrival window with you, come in ahead of the guests and have the wall up in about ten minutes. At the end of the night we take it down and leave the space as we found it.

What if the venue runs late?

Takedown at the end of the night is part of the price, including after midnight.

Welcome mirror and sign

Does the LED mirror need an outlet?

No. It runs on its own portable power station, so it can stand anywhere in the venue.

How big is the mirror?

It is a full-length arch, 20 inches wide. Most rental welcome mirrors are 16 inches. See the mirror.

Can I rent the mirror without a wall?

Yes. On its own it is $275, delivered, styled, set up and taken down in Mississauga and Brampton. Further out, travel is folded into one price.

Can you deliver just the welcome sign?

Not on its own. It comes styled with a wall or mirror booking for $125, or you can buy it for $140 and pick up the board and a flat-pack easel in Mississauga. See the sign.

Can I get the mirror or sign without the styling?

The mirror, and the sign when it comes with a booking, always arrive styled with florals, LED pillar candles, a gold lantern, greenery and chiffon. There is no bare version.
