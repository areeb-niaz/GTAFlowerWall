Questions

The things people ask before they book.

Everything below is how we already run the day. If your question is not here, call or text and we will answer it straight.

What you get

What does the day rate include?

Delivery anywhere in Toronto and the GTA, setup before the ceremony, and takedown after, with placement planned around the prayer hall layout. There are no added fees on the day.

What is the difference between Essential and Signature?

Essential is the flower wall backdrop with full draping, plus two chair covers. Signature adds a white rug under the wall, candle accents and floral arrangements. Delivery, setup and takedown are included in both. You can see them side by side on the pricing section.

How much does it cost?

Two packages, each charged as one flat day rate, with delivery, setup and takedown included. Current rates for both are listed on the pricing section of the main page.

What does the wall look like?

White cascading florals with pink and ivory accents, and chiffon drape running to the floor. There are photos from real setups in the gallery.

Delivery, setup and takedown

Do you deliver to my venue?

We cover the whole Greater Toronto Area and the corridor west to Hamilton — Toronto — including Etobicoke, Scarborough and North York — along with Mississauga, Brampton, Markham, Vaughan, Richmond Hill, Oakville, Milton, Pickering, Ajax, Burlington and Hamilton.

Anywhere in that range, delivery is included in the day rate with no added fee. Further out than that, ask us about travel and we will quote it before you book.

We have set up at ISNA Canada in Mississauga and Artistic Decor Venue in Etobicoke, and the wall works the same in a masjid prayer hall or a banquet hall.

Do I need to set anything up or take it down myself?

No. We agree an arrival window with you, come in ahead of the guests, position the wall and secure the draping. Afterwards we take the wall down and leave the space as we found it. Nothing is left for you or your family to clean up.

When do you arrive?

At an arrival window agreed with you in advance, ahead of the guests, so the wall is standing and dressed before anyone is seated.

Will the wall damage the venue?

No. It is freestanding on a narrow base, and nothing is fixed to walls, carpet or fittings. There is nothing to patch, unscrew or clean off after we leave.

How is it placed in a prayer hall?

We position it to respect the prayer hall layout and keep the walkways clear. Because it is freestanding, the placement can be adjusted on the day to suit the room.

On the day

Can the same wall be the partition and the photo backdrop?

Yes, that is what it is built for. It stands as a full-height partition separating the ceremony space during the nikkah, then becomes the wall everyone photographs afterwards. It reads clean in photos and video, in daylight or in hall lighting.

Does it close the room in?

No. It separates the ceremony space without shutting the room down — full height for privacy, but light and open in the way it reads.

How do I book a date?

Pick your date on the booking form and it fills in the request, or call or text 647-471-2148. We reply with availability, usually the same day.
